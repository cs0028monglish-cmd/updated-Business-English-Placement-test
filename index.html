How the test works

Four parts, in this order. Your examiner reviews writing and speaking and confirms your course.

  1. 1Reading and grammar. 30 questions, from easy to hard. Choose the correct answer; answer every question you can.
  2. 2Listening. One short exercise at your reading level. Press Play; you can listen three times.
  3. 3Writing. One business writing task at your level. Type it, or upload a photo of your handwriting.
  4. 4Speaking. A short conversation with our AI examiner. The questions adapt to your answers.
